## Before the Lab

Bring a rough map or list of everywhere you have physically been this
semester so far.

## In the Lab

Plan one outing beyond your usual radius: a stated activity, a stated date
and time, and at least one non-CS person invited to attend. This Lab does
not run the outing — it books it, so Assignment 2's field study has
somewhere to happen.

## Afterwards

Keep the booking. Assignment 2 (Touch Grass Field Study), due week 8, marks
the outing this books.
