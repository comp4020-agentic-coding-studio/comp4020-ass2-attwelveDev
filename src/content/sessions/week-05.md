Bring an outfit you'd actually wear outdoors — Activity 1 leaves the
room, rain or shine.

**Introduction.** Last week's plan lived entirely indoors — a sleep
schedule, an exercise plan, nothing that required leaving the building.
This week's item does: one outing, planned for real, with someone who
has never sat through a lecture of this course.

**Activity 1 (pairs, outdoors, 15 min): Field observation.** Leave the
room. Record three specific, non-screen observations: one thing seen, one
thing heard, and one brief interaction with another person, however
small.

**Return, discussion:** Share one observation each.

**Activity 2 (groups of 4): Field guide.** Groups compile the class's
observations into a short "field guide to outside" — three tips for
someone who has never been outdoors before.

**Plan your outing (pairs).** Plan one outing beyond your usual radius: a
stated activity, a stated date and time, and at least two non-CS people
invited to attend. Show the plan to your pair; they check the date,
time, and activity are all specific, and that the invited people are
named, not "some friends." This Lab does not run the outing — it gives
you a head start on one, a candidate you could use for
[Assignment 2](/assessments/assignment-2-touch-grass/)'s field study, or
swap out for something else entirely before week 8.

**Wrap-up:** Keep the plan as an option. Use it for
[Assignment 2](/assessments/assignment-2-touch-grass/), due week 8, or
plan something else by then.
