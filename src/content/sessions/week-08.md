## Before the Lab

Bring one plan from the last month that fell through or ran smaller than
intended.

## In the Lab

Write it up as an incident report: what was agreed, what actually
happened, and a root cause traced to the coordination mechanism — which
chat, which person routed messages, which step had no one responsible for
it — rather than to any one person's character.

## Afterwards

Propose one structural fix your friend group could adopt: a single
channel, a rotating organiser, a standing weekly slot. It does not need to
be adopted, only proposed.
