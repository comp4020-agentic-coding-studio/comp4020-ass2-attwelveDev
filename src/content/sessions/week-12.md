## Before the Lab

Bring every schedule and plan built across the semester: the
[week 2](/sessions/week-02/) hygiene schedule, the
[week 4](/sessions/week-04/) sleep and exercise plan, the
[week 10](/sessions/week-10/) shopping schedule, and anything else still
running — plus your Week 1 self-assessment scores.

## In the Lab

| Time | Block |
| --- | --- |
| 0:00–0:05 | Introduction — this week's topic, tied back to last Lab |
| 0:05–0:15 | Activity 1 (individual, then pairs): Then and now |
| 0:15–0:25 | Presentation / class discussion of Activity 1: Showcase |
| 0:25–0:50 | Activity 2: Assignment 3 component draft + Final Exam station rehearsal |
| 0:50–0:55 | Discussion / debrief of Activity 2 |
| 0:55–1:00 | Wrap-up |

**Introduction.** Last week's discipline was cutting a message down to
what actually mattered. This week does the same thing to twelve weeks:
comparing where you started against where you are now, and keeping only
the parts backed by something you actually did.

**Activity 1 (individual, then pairs, 10 min): Then and now.**
Individually, pull up the five scores from Week 1's starting point
check, alongside the schedules and plans you brought. Pick the one
that's changed the most, and back it up with evidence from what you
brought — something you actually did this semester, not something you
meant to. Then compare picks with one other student.

**Presentation (groups of 4): Showcase.** Each group presents one
member's biggest change as a short case study, in the same shape as
[Week 8](/sessions/week-08/)'s incident report — starting state, what
changed, and the actual reason — except this time the outcome is good.

**Activity 2: Assignment 3 component draft + Final Exam station
rehearsal.** Pick one [Assignment 3](/assessments/assignment-3-adulting/)
component you haven't drafted yet, and draft it in full using what you
already have. Then rehearse one Final Exam station under its stated time
limit, with a tutor timing it.

**Discussion / debrief:** Drafts stay yours — nobody swaps assignment
work. Instead, discuss the exam station rehearsal: name one thing the
time limit made you skip that you wouldn't skip given longer, and one
way to guard against skipping it for real.

**Wrap-up.** Read the course's opening line aloud once, as a class,
without further comment: "A practical course in the personal
maintenance a computer science degree assumes you handled elsewhere:
showering on a schedule, eating something with more than one food
group, replying to a message before it's a week old, and holding a
conversation that isn't about your degree."

## Afterwards

Keep the draft; it is the starting point for one component of what
[Assignment 3](/assessments/assignment-3-adulting/) asks you to submit
in full. Draft the rest before the deadline.
