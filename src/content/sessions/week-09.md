## Before the Lab

Bring a draft opening message if you have one; if not, bring a profile
you'd realistically message and draft one in the room.

## In the Lab

Edit the draft down to a single answerable question, then read the
edited version to a peer and ask what they would actually reply. Anything
that gets "I don't really know how to respond to that" goes back for
another cut.

## Afterwards

Nothing is submitted. What leaves the Lab is a message you would actually
send, and a peer's honest read on whether it invites a reply.
