## Before the Lab

Bring nothing prepared. The point of a starting check-in is that it happens
before any changes, so arriving having already fixed something you noticed
in the lecture defeats it.

## In the Lab

Run the check-in: a short, private self-assessment across hygiene, dress,
sleep, conversation and money, scored against no rubric but your own
honest observation. Nominate the area you will prioritise first.

## Afterwards

Nothing is submitted from this Lab. The check-in is yours to keep; the area
you nominated is the one the first few weeks of Labs lean on.
