## Before the Lab

Bring the transcript from this week's lecture reflection, or one of your
own if you have not started it yet.

## In the Lab

Take three stalled exchanges — "yeah, good", "not much", "same as usual" —
and write a follow-up line for each that a reasonable person could actually
answer. Test one in a real conversation before next week.

## Afterwards

Report back next Lab on whether your follow-up line kept the conversation
running, or stalled it at the next beat instead.
