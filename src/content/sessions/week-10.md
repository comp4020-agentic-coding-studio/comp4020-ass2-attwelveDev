## Before the Lab

Bring your last month of grocery or household receipts, or your best
estimate of what you buy and how often.

## In the Lab

Calculate your own reorder point for at least three household items, and
set a standing schedule — a recurring order or a recurring trip — that
would have avoided your most recent rush purchase.

## Afterwards

Keep the schedule. Assignment 3's grocery logistics and budget components
both draw on the reorder points calculated here.
