## Before the Lab

Bring your last month of grocery or household receipts, or your best
estimate of what you buy and how often.

## In the Lab

Work out, for at least three household items, how long you actually have
before you run out, and set a standing schedule — a recurring order or a
recurring trip — that would have avoided your most recent rush purchase.

## Afterwards

Keep the schedule. Assignment 3's grocery and budget components both draw
on the numbers calculated here.
