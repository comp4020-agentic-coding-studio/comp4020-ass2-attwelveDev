Bring your nominated area from week 1's check-in, if it was hygiene;

**Introduction.** Last week ended with a scoring exercise and a nominated
priority. This week the point is to turn that into something with an
actual shape — a schedule you could hand to someone else and have them
follow, not just a good intention.

**Activity 1 (groups of 3): Maintenance calendar.** Each group drafts a
shared seven-day grid covering four columns:

1. Shower
2. Hair wash
3. Laundry
4. Haircut due (a reminder column, not a task)

Balance a frequency that's realistic against what the group agrees a
plausible first-year student would actually manage.

**Presentation:** One group's calendar goes on the board for the rest of
the class to critique the frequencies chosen.

**Activity 2: Peer check.** Individually and privately, fill in your own
real version of the calendar: a frequency for every routine task, plus a
stated fallback for the week it does not go to plan. Tutors check
schedules for a fallback before anything else — a schedule that only works
when nothing goes wrong is not a schedule.

**Discussion / debrief (pairs):** Swap your filled-in calendar with one
other student for one honest, one-line comment only — no further detail
required.

**Wrap-up:** This calendar isn't a one-off — it's what week 4's
[Assignment 1](/assessments/assignment-1-makeover/) draws its hygiene
product selection from, so keep it somewhere you can actually find again.
