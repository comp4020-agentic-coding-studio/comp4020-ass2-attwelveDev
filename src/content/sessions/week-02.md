## Before the Lab

Bring your nominated area from week 1's check-in, if it was hygiene;
otherwise bring an honest account of your current hygiene routine as it
actually runs.

## In the Lab

Build a hygiene maintenance schedule: every routine task, its frequency,
and a stated fallback for the week it does not go to plan. Tutors check
schedules for a fallback before anything else — a schedule that only works
when nothing goes wrong is not a schedule.

## Afterwards

Keep the schedule. It is the artefact week 4's Assignment 1 draws its
hygiene product selection from.
