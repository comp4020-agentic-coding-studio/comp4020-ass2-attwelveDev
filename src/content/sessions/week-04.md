**Introduction.** Last week's ranking exercise showed the same outfit
landing differently depending on the occasion — the fit was never fixed,
only the occasion changed. This week runs the same test on a schedule:
not whether the plan sounds good, but whether it survives a week that
doesn't cooperate.

**Activity 1 (groups of 4): Jordan's Week case study.** Each group is
handed the following case study:

> **Jordan's Week**
>
> Monday: Plan says asleep by 11:30pm. Actual: still awake at 3am
> finishing an assignment. Plan says gym at 7am. Actual: alarm snoozed
> four times, gym skipped.
>
> Tuesday: "Will sleep early tonight," typed into a note that is never
> looked at again. Stayed up until 2am watching one more episode.
> Exercise logged as "30 min run"; actually walked to the vending
> machine and back twice.
>
> Wednesday: Skipped breakfast, ate two-minute noodles at 1pm and again
> at 9pm. Told a friend "I've been so good with food lately."
>
> Thursday: Went to bed on time for the first time all week. Woke up
> anyway at 4am to check a phone notification and stayed up.
>
> Friday: No plan was made for this day at all.
>
> Saturday: Slept until 2pm to "catch up," then couldn't sleep until 4am
> that night.
>
> Sunday: Wrote next week's plan. It is identical to this week's plan.
>
> Jordan's week contains six stated intentions and zero actual times.

Groups analyse what's realistic, what's just wishful thinking, what
fails first, and agree on the single most useful fix.

**Presentation:** Each group presents their one best fix only.

**Activity 2: Build your own plan.** Individually, extend your week 2
hygiene schedule with the same model: a sleep schedule with a target
bedtime and a fallback for the night it slips, and an exercise plan with
a stated frequency and a named activity — not "sleep more" or "exercise
more." A real plan needs a time, not an intention.

**Discussion / debrief (pairs):** Show your sleep schedule and exercise
plan to one other student; they reject anything in either that reads as
an intention rather than a stated time.

**Wrap-up:** Keep your sleep schedule and exercise plan; revise either
before [Assignment 3](/assessments/assignment-3-adulting/) asks for
updated versions of both, marked in week 12.
