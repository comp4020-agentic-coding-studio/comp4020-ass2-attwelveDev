## Before the Lab

Bring your group chat's timestamps from the last week, or your best honest
estimate of when you are actually online.

## In the Lab

Design one mechanism — physical, not a rule you tell yourself — that
enforces a device-free window: where the laptop physically goes, what
happens if it doesn't go there, and the one allowed exception for a
genuine deadline.

## Afterwards

Run the mechanism for a week before week 7's Lab. Bring back whether it
held, and if not, where it failed.
