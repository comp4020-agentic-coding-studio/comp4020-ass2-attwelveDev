Recorded, over eleven years of dating in three cities, approximately two
hundred first dates and four second dates --- a ratio now cited in their
own teaching materials as Exhibit A, not an anomaly. Holds a state debate
championship title, a credential they maintain transfers directly to
small talk and which the rest of the teaching team maintains does not.

Teaches conversation as a skill like any other: the two-minute silence
after "how was your weekend?", the four-draft email, the 600-word opening
message. Runs the friendship, small-talk and dating Labs and administers the
Final Exam's small-talk and Reading the Room stations.
