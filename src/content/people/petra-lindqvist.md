Teaches domestic logistics as a scheduling problem: a meal plan is a resource
allocation exercise, a sleep schedule is a maintenance window, and a grocery
run is a supply chain with a two-week lead time if you plan it badly. Runs the
sleep, nutrition and budgeting Labs and administers the Final Exam's daily
survival station.
