Held a Michelin star for four consecutive years at a twelve-seat
restaurant before resigning to teach eighteen-year-olds how to keep a
fridge stocked. Their signature fourteen-ingredient ragù has been
formally adapted for this course into a single approved instruction: buy
the sauce, but buy it on Tuesday.

Teaches domestic logistics as a scheduling problem: a meal plan is a budget
with a shopping list attached, a sleep schedule needs real time set aside,
and a grocery run has to happen before, not after, you're out of something.
Runs the sleep, nutrition and budgeting Labs and administers the Final
Exam's daily survival station.
