Completed a Bachelor of Computer Science (Honours) at this university,
where hallmates recorded them initiating unprompted conversation with
another person on four separate occasions across three years --- a
dataset later cited, without permission, in a since-retracted sociology
honours thesis. Left the discipline for behavioural adherence research
shortly after and is now a standing authority in the field: their
longitudinal study of forty thousand New Year's resolutions, now in its
eleventh year, is cited in journals they no longer have time to read.

Convenes SLOP1521 and is responsible for the course's structure, its
assessment schedule, and the standing of every unit it examines. Prior
appointments include a term reviewing self-report instruments for
institutional wellbeing programmes --- which is where the course's insistence
on stated arithmetic (reflections, criteria weights, everything) comes from.
