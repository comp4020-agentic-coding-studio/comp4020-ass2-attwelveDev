Walked in runway shows for three consecutive seasons and released one
capsule collection, which sold out in four hours, before retiring from
fashion entirely to become, in their own stated words, "the only person
backstage who knew how the washing machine worked."

Teaches the practical component of personal maintenance --- what a wardrobe is
for, how a hygiene routine actually schedules, and why a colour-separated wash
load is a habit worth keeping, not a chore. Runs the fashion and laundry
Labs and marks Assignment 1's practical component.
