## The brief

> Leave your usual 400-metre radius and do one thing that isn't computer
> science, with people who also aren't computer science.

Pick one activity from the seven on offer, run it for real, and write it
up in a 1000-word report.

## The seven activities

Choose one: a park, a café, team sports, a museum, a social event,
shopping, or a club.

## The three restrictions

1. **No laptop, no coding.** The activity is not computer science wearing
   a costume.
2. **CS discussion capped at five minutes.** If the conversation drifts
   toward anything you would bring up in a lecture, redirect it — and if it
   runs past five minutes in total, say so in the report.
3. **At least two non-CS people.** Name them (first names are enough) and
   how you know them.

## Compliance is self-reported

Nobody is following you around with a stopwatch. Compliance with all three
restrictions is self-reported, and the report is the evidence: write it as
if it will be checked against a recording, because for marking purposes it
is the recording.

## What you submit

A 1000-word report naming the activity, when it ran, and who else was
there, addressing each of the three restrictions directly.

The report is submitted on paper. See [Policies](/policies/) for where
to hand it in and the extenuating-circumstances exception.
