## Introduction

Would you wear what you're wearing right now to a job interview? No need
to answer aloud. Clothing measurably changes how a wearer thinks and acts,
not just how they're perceived — today's scope is occasion-matching and
basic colour coordination only, the one topic in the course with genuinely
surprising research behind it.

## Body

- **Clothing changes cognition, not just perception.** Adam, H., &
  Galinsky, A. D. (2012) found a coat described as a doctor's lab coat
  measurably improved sustained attention versus the same coat described
  as a painter's coat — the effect needs both the physical experience of
  wearing it and its symbolic meaning.

<CheckIn question="Name the two factors enclothed cognition depends on." />

- **Reading an occasion.** The five outfit photos from the Week 3 lab each
  have a best-fit occasion — the same item can be right or wrong depending
  on what it's read against, not on the item alone.

<CheckIn question="What's the one detail that would flip an outfit from interview-appropriate to gym-appropriate?" />

- **Assembling from what you own.** A wardrobe only has to cover a small
  number of recurring occasion types, not one item per occasion —
  Assignment 1 previews the same task.
