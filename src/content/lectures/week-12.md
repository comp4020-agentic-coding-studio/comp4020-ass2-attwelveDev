## Overview

Twelve weeks of separate routines converge this week: the same person,
running all of them at once, for real. Nothing new is introduced — the
work from here is keeping everything running together, not adding more.

## Content

- reviewing all five recurring topics as one schedule competing for the
  same hours, rather than five separate ones
- what changed, topic by topic, since week 1's starting inventory
- consolidating everything into the week-long plan Assignment 3 asks for
- what the Final Exam's five stations examine, station by station

## Case study

The same desk from week 1, photographed again: the accumulated clutter is
gone, replaced by a shallow tray for the items that used to sit loose on
the surface — not tidiness as an event, but a system that now has
somewhere for things to go. The sock pile from week 2 is down from
twenty-three unpaired socks to four, the remainder tracked against a
laundry schedule that finally states a frequency. Neither photograph is
dramatic. Both are what eleven weeks of actual maintenance look like.

## Reflection

This week's prompt is the twelfth and final one, set on the same schedule
as the rest — but it is not graded on its own; it feeds directly into
Assignment 3's daily-routine component instead.

## Assessment tie-in

Assignment 3 (Time to be an Adult) is due this Friday at 12:00, and the
Final Exam follows on Wednesday 9 June. Between them, every topic this
course has covered is assessed once more, together.
