## Overview

An outfit is the output of a system whose input is the occasion. Fashion
Fundamentals treats "what to wear" as a lookup problem: given an occasion,
what does the wardrobe actually have on hand, and does it match.

## Content

- reading an occasion for its actual constraints, not its vibe
- building a wardrobe that covers a small number of recurring occasion
  types, rather than one item per occasion
- laundry frequency as a hard constraint on wardrobe size
- justifying a choice in writing, the way Assignment 1 will ask you to

## Case study

A free conference t-shirt, worn to a wedding. The shirt was clean, the
event was formal, and the wearer owned exactly one collared shirt, which
was in the laundry. The failure was not the t-shirt; it was a wardrobe with
no second option for an occasion type it had already failed once before.
