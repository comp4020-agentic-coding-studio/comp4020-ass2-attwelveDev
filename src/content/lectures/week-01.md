## Overview

Every `subsystem` you are about to run for the next twelve weeks is already
running, right now, whether or not anyone taught you to run it. This course
treats that as the starting condition, not the problem.

## Content

- the course's five recurring `subsystems`: hygiene, dress, sleep,
  conversation and money
- what a baseline diagnostic is, and why week 1 takes one before anything
  is taught
- how the weekly shape works: a lecture states the theory, the Lab runs it
- the assessment scheme, in outline: five items, always weighted, always
  visible

## Case study

Your desk, as it exists right now, is this course's first dataset. Before
you tidy anything, take stock of what is actually on it: what has
accumulated by habit, what stays out because putting it away costs more
effort than leaving it, what you have stopped seeing entirely. Week 12
returns to this exact desk.

## Reflection

Due 12:00 Tuesday of week 2: name one thing that is currently running on
you unmonitored — a habit, a workaround, a piece of `unscheduled downtime`
you have stopped noticing — and describe what it would take to observe it
properly for a week.

## Assessment tie-in

Weekly Reflections opens this week. Your first prompt — the one above — is
due 12:00 the following Tuesday, and the same schedule runs for the rest of
semester.
