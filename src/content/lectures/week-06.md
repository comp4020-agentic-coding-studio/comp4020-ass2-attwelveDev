## Overview

A rule like "no laptop after dinner" describes an intention, not a
mechanism. Without something that actually enforces the boundary, the
laptop stays open for the same reason it always did.

## Content

- separating a device from a room, rather than trusting willpower to
  separate a habit from a device
- what an hour with the laptop closed actually gets filled with, and
  whether that is planned or accidental
- reading your own group chats as a log of when boundaries actually held
- a manual override for the one evening a real deadline needs the laptop
  open anyway

## Case study

A class group chat, sorted by timestamp and filtered to messages sent
between 11pm and 2am — the exact hours a "no laptops after dinner" rule was
supposed to cover. The chat was busiest in that window every night of the
week it was logged. The rule had never been broken by anyone typing "I
know I shouldn't be on this" — it had simply never been enforced by
anything other than the rule existing.
