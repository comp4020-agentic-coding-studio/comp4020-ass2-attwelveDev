## Overview

A hygiene routine is only as good as what happens the week everything else
goes wrong. Most advice on the subject assumes you just need to want it
more, and skips the part where you plan for the bad week.

## Content

- doing the routine task versus fixing the mess after it slips, applied to
  hygiene
- building a routine that survives a bad week, not just a good one
- the actual ways it slips: a skipped day, missing supplies, no backup plan
- reading your own hygiene routine honestly, not how you'd like it to look

## Case study

Twenty-three unpaired socks, collected from under a bed, a desk, and the
back seat of a car, photographed together. Every one of them is proof that
laundry got deferred past the point a matching pair survived. Week 12
returns to this exact pile.

## Reflection

Due 12:00 Tuesday of week 3: describe what actually went wrong with your
hygiene routine this week — not the routine you intend to run, the one
that actually happened — and name the smallest change that would have
prevented it.
