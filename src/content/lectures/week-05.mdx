## Introduction

Memory and attention performance improved by roughly 20% after an hour
interacting with nature, in a real study. "Go outside" has an actual,
measured mechanism behind it — today's scope is attention restoration
only; general physical-health benefits are out of scope.

## Body

- **Attention restoration, defined plainly.** Berman, M. G., Jonides, J.,
  & Kaplan, S. (2008) found walking in a natural setting — or even viewing
  pictures of nature — improved directed-attention task performance
  versus a busy urban walk.

<CheckIn question="Would a walk through a busy street give you the same benefit as a walk through a park, according to this study?" />

- **Why this isn't about "fresh air."** A park walk and a street walk can
  be the same exercise, at the same pace, breathing the same outdoor
  air — the attentional effect still differs. It's the setting doing the
  work, not the oxygen.
- **What the lab will ask you to do.** The Week 5 lab runs the outdoor
  field observation directly off this finding.
