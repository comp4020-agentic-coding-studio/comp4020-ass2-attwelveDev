## Definitions

- **Apology** (noun) — a statement acknowledging fault, distinct from a statement acknowledging that the other person has feelings.
- **Group chat** (noun) — a shared messaging thread among several people, notable for periods of total silence followed by sudden, simultaneous activity.
- **Listening to someone** (verb) — to give conscious attention to someone speaking, not while thinking about video games or your coding project.

## Key point 1 — The six components of an effective apology

Lewicki, R. J., Polin, B., & Lount, R. B. (2016). *An Exploration of the Structure of Effective Apologies.* Negotiation and Conflict Management Research, 9(2), 177–196.

Expression of regret. Explanation. Acknowledgment of responsibility. Declaration of repentance. Offer of repair. Request for forgiveness.

## Key point 2 — Which components matter most

Acknowledgment of responsibility mattered most.

Then offer of repair.

Request for forgiveness mattered least.
