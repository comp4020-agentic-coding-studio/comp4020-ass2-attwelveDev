## Definitions

- **Outfit** (noun) — a coordinated set of garments selected for a specific occasion, as distinct from "whatever was on the floor."
- **Dress code** (noun) — an unwritten or written expectation for attire at a given event, violation of which is noticed by everyone except the violator.
- **Occasion** (noun) — an event with its own implicit clothing requirements, distinct from "any day that ends in Y."

## Key point 1 — Clothing changes cognition, not just perception

Adam, H., & Galinsky, A. D. (2012). *Enclothed cognition.* Journal of Experimental Social Psychology, 48(4), 918–925.

A coat described as a doctor's lab coat measurably improved sustained attention versus the same coat described as a painter's coat.

The effect needs both the physical experience of wearing it and its symbolic meaning.
