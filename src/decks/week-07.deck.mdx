## Definitions

- **Small talk** (noun) — brief, low-stakes conversation exchanged with someone who is not a close friend, typically covering weather, weekend plans, or transit delays.
- **Eye contact** (noun) — the act of looking at another person's eyes during conversation, for a duration long enough to signal attention and short enough to avoid alarm.

## Key point 1 — The actual comfortable length of eye contact

Binetti, N., Harrison, C., Coutrot, A., Johnston, A., & Mareschal, I. (2016). *Pupil dilation as an index of preferred mutual gaze duration.* Royal Society Open Science, 3(7), 160086.

Average preferred mutual gaze duration was around 3.2–3.3 seconds.

Consistent across a wide international sample.

## Key point 2 — Silence is not a failure state

A pause doesn't mean the conversation has gone wrong.

## Key point 3 — The small-talk formula

Name. One hobby. One recent trip.

Three fixed slots, filled in any order.

---

## In-lecture activity

In pairs with a neighbour: hold eye contact for a silently counted three seconds.

Then immediately use the small-talk formula once.
