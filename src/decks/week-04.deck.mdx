## Definitions

- **Bedtime** (noun) — a specific, named clock time at which one intends to be asleep, not merely "later."
- **Balanced meal** (noun) — a plate containing more than one food group, particularly one that is not currently instant noodles.
- **Exercise** (noun) — physical activity undertaken on a stated schedule, distinguished from "the walk to the vending machine."

## Key point 1 — How much sleep you actually need

Hirshkowitz, M., Whiton, K., Albert, S. M., et al. (2015). *National Sleep Foundation's sleep time duration recommendations: methodology and results summary.* Sleep Health, 1(1), 40–43.

Seven to nine hours recommended for adults 18–64.

## Key point 3 — Exercise as a frequency

Chekroud, S. R., Gueorguieva, R., Zheutlin, A. B., Paulus, M., Krumholz, H. M., Krystal, J. H., & Chekroud, A. M. (2018). *Association between physical exercise and mental health in 1.2 million individuals in the USA between 2011 and 2015.* The Lancet Psychiatry, 5(9), 739–746.

1.49 fewer poor-mental-health days per month than non-exercisers. Benefits peaked around 45 minutes, 3–5 times a week.

More than 3 hours a day was associated with worse outcomes than none at all.
