## Key point 2 — What actually changed, and how we know

The Then and Now method: pull up the five scores from Week 1's starting point check, alongside every schedule and plan built since.

Pick the one area that changed the most, and back it up with something you actually did this semester — the same starting-state/what-changed/actual-reason shape as Week 8's incident report, except this time the outcome is good.

The point: a self-report alone can't tell "I got better at this" apart from "I've decided I got better at this." A schedule you actually kept can.

This week's lab runs the method itself, using the scores and schedules you bring with you.

<CheckIn question="Between a self-report and a kept schedule, which one can actually tell you apart from someone who just decided they'd improved?" />
