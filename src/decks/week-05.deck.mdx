## Definitions

- **Outdoors** (noun) — the region beyond a building's exterior walls, accessible via a door.
- **Sunlight** (noun) — direct illumination from the sun, typically encountered before 2pm.
- **Fresh air** (noun) — air that has not previously circulated through a shared bedroom for several consecutive days.

## Key point 1 — Attention restoration, defined plainly

Berman, M. G., Jonides, J., & Kaplan, S. (2008). *The cognitive benefits of interacting with nature.* Psychological Science, 19(12), 1207–1212.

Walking in a natural setting — or even viewing pictures of nature — improved directed-attention task performance versus a busy urban walk.

<CheckIn question="Would a walk through a busy street give you the same benefit as a walk through a park, according to this study?" />

## Key point 2 — Why this isn't about "fresh air"

Same exercise. Same pace. Same outdoor air.

The attentional effect still differs — it's the setting doing the work, not the oxygen.
