# SLOP1521

## Introduction to Life: Personal Systems Maintenance

Week 1 — Orientation

---

{/* _class: impact */}

Competence is a skill like any other. CS culture just never taught you this one.

---

## What this course is

Hygiene, sleep, dress, conversation and money, taught with the full
institutional seriousness of a real prerequisite: scheduling, root-cause
analysis and regression testing, applied to a human being.

Assessed by practical examination, the same as anything else on your
transcript.

---

## The five recurring subsystems

- hygiene
- dress
- sleep
- conversation
- money

Every one of them is already running on you. This course does not install
them; it gives them a maintenance schedule.

---

## The weekly shape

Every week, the same five slots, in the same order:

1. Overview
2. Content
3. Case study
4. Reflection
5. Assessment tie-in

A lecture states the theory. The Lab runs it. Twelve weeks, one shape,
no exceptions.

---

## The assessment scheme

| Item | Weight |
| --- | --- |
| Weekly Reflections | 15% |
| Assignment 1: Makeover | 15% |
| Assignment 2: Touch Grass Field Study | 20% |
| Assignment 3: Time to be an Adult | 20% |
| Final Exam | 30% |

Every item is weighted, always visible, always adding up to 100.

---

## Today's baseline diagnostic

Before this course changes anything, it measures what is already there.

In today's Lab: a short, honest self-assessment across all five
subsystems, and one subsystem nominated as your first priority.

No rubric. No submission. Just a baseline to measure against.

```notes
Give the room five uninterrupted minutes for the diagnostic before asking
anyone to nominate a subsystem out loud.
```

---

## Due before week 2's lecture

- your first Weekly Reflection: due 12:00 Tuesday of week 2
- name one thing currently running on you unmonitored

Week 2 starts with hygiene as a scheduled subsystem. Bring an honest
account of your current routine.
